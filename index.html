Core performance insights – what worked brilliantly
  • Brand trust as #1 driver: “Trust in Bayer” cited as top purchase reason in ~90% of sessions, reinforcing brand equity and beating price-only competitors.
  • Education drives conversion: 97% clarity directly linked to 88% definite use intent; awareness turned into commitment.
  • Influencer network built: 200+ Farmer Captains identified for testimonials and peer advocacy.
  • Timing & credibility: Pre-sowing sessions delivered by field teams and dealers created memorable, credible education.
Critical opportunities & barriers
  • Price sensitivity among ~12% hesitant farmers, especially in regions like Mianwali.
  • Data gaps for Sargodha/Phalia and some later sessions – require tighter tracking for full insight.
  • High farmer demand for physical demo plots as proof points.
  • Need regional customisation – e.g. focus on pricing narratives where resistance is highest.

Key Insights, Business Impact & Next Steps
Top success factors
  • Brand trust leader“Trust in Bayer” is the #1 purchase reason.
  • Education excellence97% message clarity – farmers clearly understand dose, timing and safety.
  • Strong conversion88% definite use intent among engaged farmers.
  • Network built200+ Farmer Captains ready for advocacy and referrals.
Farmer requests & opportunities
  • High demand for demo plots in key villages as physical proof of performance.
  • 45+ farmers willing to share experiences on social media / WhatsApp.
  • Competitor mentions (Evyol, Suncrop) monitored for benchmarking.
Business impact assessment
  • Market penetration: 26 targeted education sessions addressing 19,025+ acres.
  • Brand equity reinforcement: trust-first model differentiates Buctril from price-only competitors.
  • Conversion pipeline: 88% intent gives strong seasonal forecast accuracy.
  • Competitive advantage: farmer education plus safety benefits create moat vs generics.
Brand equity dashboard
  • Salience – Market reach: 1,123 farmers ✅
  • Performance – Clarity: 97% understanding ✅
  • Imagery – Purchase driver: “Trust in Bayer”.
  • Judgements – Conversion: 88% definite use ✅
  • Feelings – Confidence: high confidence reported across sessions ✅
Challenges & price sensitivity
  • Price seen as high by 15–20% of hesitant farmers; more pronounced in some pockets (e.g. Mianwali).
  • Credit constraints and delayed payments noted; require dealer schemes / micro-packs.
  • Partial data for some sessions – tracking must be tightened for complete view.
Recommended marketing actions
  • Immediate (next 30 days): launch micro-packs, establish five demo plots (Ubaro, Ghotki, Daud Khail and similar high-conversion villages), activate top 20 influencers with testimonials.
  • Next season: formalise “Farmer Captain” program, run pre-sowing refresher sessions, build local-language visual guides for dose and water volume.
  • Longer term: strengthen dealer feedback loop, build digital insight repository, expand to new territories using this engagement template.
Growth template – the Buctril engagement framework
  • Timing: align sessions with pre-sowing decision window.
  • Clarity: keep 2–3 simple, memorable key messages.
  • Credibility: field team expertise plus dealer partners.
  • Community: local influencers driving peer-to-peer recommendation.
  • Consistency: standardised session structure across regions.
  • Feedback loop: capture and act on farmer concerns in near-real time.
Final verdict
  • Overall rating: 8.5 / 10.
  • Key insight: education builds trust, and trust drives conversion.
  • Next priority: address price sensitivity and credit barriers to capture the remaining ~12% hesitant farmers.
