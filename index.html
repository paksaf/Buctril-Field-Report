Key Insights, Business Impact & Next Steps

Brand trust leader Education excellence Strong conversion intent Network of influencers

Top success factors

  • Brand trust leader: “Trust in Bayer” as primary purchase reason.
  • Education excellence: simple key messages driving high clarity.
  • Strong conversion: high definite-use intent from engaged farmers.
  • Network built: Farmer Captains ready for advocacy and referrals.

Farmer requests & opportunities

  • High demand for demo plots in key villages as physical proof of performance.
  • Farmers willing to share experiences on social media / WhatsApp.
  • Competitor mentions monitored for benchmarking.

Business impact assessment

  • Market penetration: targeted sessions addressing large wheat acres.
  • Brand equity reinforcement: trust-first model differentiates from price-only competitors.
  • Conversion pipeline: intent supports strong seasonal forecast accuracy.
  • Competitive advantage: education + safety benefits vs generics.

Challenges & price sensitivity

Challenge Impact Action Required
Price sensitivity Medium Micro-packs and tailored dealer schemes.
Data gaps Low Improve tracking for missing days & locations.
Competition Low Continue education-first differentiation vs generics.

Growth template – engagement framework

  • Timing: align sessions with pre-sowing decision window.
  • Clarity: keep 2–3 simple, memorable key messages.
  • Credibility: field team expertise plus dealer partners.
  • Community: local influencers driving recommendation.
  • Consistency: standardised session structure across regions.
  • Feedback loop: capture farmer concerns and act quickly.
