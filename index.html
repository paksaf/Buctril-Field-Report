Key Insights, Barriers & Next Steps (From Field Reports)
Strengths
  • Brand loyalty80%+ repeat users across sessions.
  • Conversion~88% farmers committed to Buctril Super.
  • ReachNearly all wheat growers touched in SKR, RYK, DGK, FSD, GUJ.
  • MessagingMessage clarity average score 2.8–2.9 / 3.0.
Why farmers choose Buctril Super
  • Trust in Bayer brand – 32 mentions.
  • Better weed control vs alternatives – 30 mentions.
  • Good past experience – 30 mentions.
  • Safe on crop (no burn) – 29 mentions.
Farmer requests & opportunities
  • High demand for demo plots in key villages.
  • 45+ farmers ready for social media advocacy.
  • Competitor mentions: Evyol & Suncrop monitored for benchmarking.
Key influencers identified
  • SKR – 12+ farmers keen on demo plots.
  • RYK – 8+ community leaders.
  • DGK – 10+ farmers active in local groups.
  • FSD – 6+ young, tech-savvy farmers.
  • Spotlight: Shat Ullah (0305-3126621) – 23 acres, ideal for testimonials.
Areas for attention
  • Price sensitivity among 15–20% of hesitant farmers.
  • Preference for generic alternatives in some pockets.
  • Credit constraints highlighted in multiple sessions.
Barriers to conversion
  • Price too high – 15 mentions.
  • Fear of crop damage – 7 mentions.
  • Happy with generic brand – 4 mentions.
  • No money / credit – 5 mentions.
Field team feedback
  • Engagement highest in live Q&A sessions.
  • Price concerns call for dealer schemes / smaller packs.
  • Timing: pre-sowing sessions drive strongest commitment.
Session effectiveness snapshot
  • Farmer awareness: ★★★★★ (90%+ awareness).
  • Message recall: ★★★★☆ (strong on safety & use).
  • Conversion intent: ★★★★★ (~88% definite usage).
  • Influencer ID: ★★★★☆ (45+ farmer influencers identified).
  • Price sensitivity: ★★☆☆☆ (needs improvement).
Recommended next steps
  • Immediate (next 30 days): 3–5 demo plots, 10–15 influencer testimonials, address pricing with dealer schemes.
  • Medium term (next season): launch “Farmer Captains”, develop local-language visuals, pre-sowing educational calendar.
  • Long term: build digital insight repository, strengthen dealer feedback loop, expand to new territories.
Conclusion
  • Impact: 19 sessions | 567 farmers | 7,265 acres represented (first phase).
  • Overall rating: 8.5 / 10 – high trust, clear messaging, strong engagement.
  • Next focus: demo plots, pricing schemes & influencer outreach to convert remaining hesitant farmers.
